# Narration lines, one line per line of speech: key|text
#
# The key becomes the file name: voice/<locale>/<key>.mp3 (or .ogg)
# Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored. Keys must be unique.
#
# One file per language - lines.txt, lines.fr.txt, lines.de.txt - each rendered
# with a voice that speaks that language, into that language's folder:
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\Render-VoiceClips.ps1 -Voice en-US-AriaNeural
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\Render-VoiceClips.ps1 -Voice fr-FR-DeniseNeural -LinesFile tools\lines.fr.txt
#
# Voice names: python -m edge_tts --list-voices
# The addon picks the folder from the client's own locale (GetLocale), so a
# French client plays voice/frFR/ and an English one voice/enUS/.
#
# The addon plays the "ratchet" clip when a flight launches.

croisee|You are gliding over the Barrens, a plateau of dry grass that was a forest ten thousand years ago, before the fall of the Well of Eternity. There, where the Gold Road crosses the trail that climbs toward Orgrimmar, the Horde planted its most precious outpost in all of Kalimdor: the Crossroads. Thrall built it after the exodus from Lordaeron, when the young Orgrimmar had to be linked to the pastures of Mulgore. It is not a city; it is a watchtower set on a crossroads — grunts, hides drying in the sun, and a single dirt track running south. But everything the Horde owns moves through here: every arms convoy, every column of troops, every message. So the enemy prowls around it — the Razormane quilboar hiding in the thorns, the harpies diving off the cliffs, and the centaurs, the tauren's ancient enemy, raiding the caravans. As long as the Crossroads holds, the Horde holds the roads. If it falls, Kalimdor is cut in two. That is why all eyes turn to it — it is the beating heart of the Barrens and the first wall of Orgrimmar.
ratchet|Here is Ratchet, the goblin port planted on the eastern coast of the Barrens. Here neither Horde nor Alliance dictates the law: the Steamwheedle Cartel rules, and the only language that counts is gold. Ratchet is the work of a single goblin: Gazlowe, the engineering genius who helped Rexxar lay the foundations of Orgrimmar during the Third War. He understood before anyone else that a neutral port in the heart of Kalimdor is worth more than an army. Goods are unloaded, sold, spied on — and everyone pays. Ships link Ratchet to Booty Bay, at the other end of the world. There was a time when it was only a smuggler's cove, a den where pirates hid their cargo under the noses of both factions. Today it is a town that never sleeps, guarded by the Cartel's goblin bruisers. Horde and Alliance merchants pass each other here without slitting each other's throats, because here money is stronger than war. Ratchet's motto fits in one line: everything is for sale, everything is bought, and no one asks questions.
